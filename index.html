FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Can't find what you need? Talk to a packaging expert — we usually reply within an hour.

What is the minimum order?

Most styles start at just 100 units. That makes it easy to test a new product or launch without filling a warehouse.

How long does it take?

Standard orders ship in 10 business days after you approve your proof. Need it sooner? Rush production in 5 days is available on most styles.

Can I get a sample first?

Yes. Order a plain sample in your exact size for $15, or a printed sample for $49. Both amounts are credited back when you place your full order.

I don't have a design. Can you help?

Absolutely. Send us your logo and colours and our in-house designers will create a layout for you, free on orders over 500 units.

Is your packaging recyclable?

All our paper and cardboard packaging is curbside recyclable. We also offer compostable mailers and plastic-free tape.
